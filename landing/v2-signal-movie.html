HOW IT WORKS

Three steps. The third one is the point.

Boring todos, gone. Today's list is a route. Crossing one off is a strike, and the strike is the scene. That's the epic part. Trust us.

1

Add a stop

Small, medium, or big. A colored line if it belongs with other stuff. Seven lines, same colors as the subway, on purpose.

2

Cross it off

Drag your finger through it. The ink follows the words. Not a checkbox. Not a swipe menu. Not “are you sure.”

Pick up dry cleaning
3

A scene plays

Three seconds, full screen, pixel. Dealt from a shuffle, so you don't get the same one back to back. Then the list is back. Next stop.

It's a number. It can go down.

Consecutive days with at least one thing crossed off. It's the only number in the app that can go down, and when it does, nothing happens. No reminder. No red badge. It sits on the strip until you're back. This is not a planned service change.

  • Quiet hours — pick a time. After it, scenes go minimal.
  • Sound — low, full or off
  • Haptics — on or off
  • Reduce Motion — respected. The scene still plays, the motion doesn't
  • Your own photo as the background
  • Two looks: Signal and Classic
