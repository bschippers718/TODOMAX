HOW IT WORKS

Three steps. The third one is the point.

Items are stops. Today's list is a route. Crossing one off is a strike, and every strike plays a scene.

1

Add a stop

Say if it's small, medium or big. If you want, give it a colored line to group it with other things. Seven lines, same as the subway.

2

Cross it off

Drag your finger through it. The ink follows across the text. Not a checkbox.

Pick up dry cleaning
3

A scene plays

Three seconds, full screen, pixel art. Dealt from a shuffle, so you don't get the same one twice in a row.

PACKS

One a day keeps it going. Miss a day and it starts over.

It's a number. It can go down.

Consecutive days with at least one thing crossed off. It's the only number in the app that can go down, and when it does, nothing happens. No reminders, no red badge on the icon. It sits on the Daily Route strip until you're back.

  • Quiet hours — after a time you pick, scenes go minimal
  • Sound — low, full or off
  • Haptics — on or off
  • Reduce Motion — respected. The scene still plays, the motion doesn't
  • Your own photo as the background
  • Two looks: Signal and Classic

No pop-ups. No badge. A number.
