What it is

1The strike

A line, not a checkbox

Drag your finger across the item. The ink follows. That's how you finish something here, not a tap.

2Celebrations

Three seconds, then it's gone

Every time you cross something off, a full-screen pixel scene plays. They shuffle, so you don't get the same one back to back.

3Daily route

Today is a route

Items are stops. See them on a map, fill in the Daily Route strip, keep the streak going.
