How it works

Three things to learn.

1

Add something.

Type it. Say whether it's small, medium or big. If it belongs with other stuff, put it on a line. The line colors the whole card.

2

Cross it off.

Drag your finger across it. The ink follows. No checkbox, no swipe menu, no "are you sure."

3

Something happens.

A three-second pixel scene, full screen. They're dealt from a shuffle, so you don't get the same one twice in a row. Then you're back on the list.

The Daily Route

Seven squares. That's your week.

One square per day, filled in if you crossed something off. None of your actual tasks are on it, so you can post it without thinking about it. The streak is consecutive days.
